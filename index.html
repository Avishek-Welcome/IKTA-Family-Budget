IKTA Family Budget 1. 🔐 User Authentication & Onboarding Google Sign-In: One-tap login via GoogleAuthHelper (maps email, display name, and photo URL). Role Assignment: Automatically assigns roles: Head of Family (Admin) or Family Member. Family Group Setup: Head of Family: Creates and administers the family group. Family Member: Requests to join via Head Email. Approval Gatekeeper: Directs unapproved members to PendingApprovalScreen until granted access. 2. 🛡️ Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) & Presets Features customizable permissions (MemberPermissions) with 6 pre-configured presets: Private Member: Manage own expenses; restricted from viewing other members' records or family totals. Family Contributor (Default): Manage own expenses; full access to shared family expenses, totals, categories, and monthly reports. Shared Financial Member: View own expenses plus specific allowed family members' expenses. Family Finance Viewer: Read-only visibility into totals, category charts, and reports; cannot add/edit entries. Restricted Access: Temporarily suspends expense entry and shared data access while keeping personal profile features. Custom Configuration: Head of Family can toggle granular permissions across 4 areas: Expense Entry: Add/Edit/Delete own or other members' expenses. Visibility: Configure access to own, Head, all members, totals, or specific allowed emails. Reports: Access category charts, monthly reports, and exports. Administration: Manage categories and approve/reject membership requests. 3. 💵 Transaction Entry & Smart Keypad Custom Keypad (CalculatorKeypadView): Built-in calculator (+, -, ×, ÷, =, %) with haptic vibration feedback on keypress. Transaction Metadata: Types: EXPENSE, INCOME, TRANSFER. Details: Custom notes, tags, receipt images, date/time pickers, and currency selector. Author Info: Captures addedByEmail, addedByName, addedByPhotoUrl, and lastModifiedTimestamp. 4. 🎨 Category Customization & Visual Styling Presets & Custom Categories: Pre-packaged categories (Shopping, Groceries, Bills, Healthcare, Transport, Salary, etc.) with custom icon keys and hex color coding. Media Attachments: Upload custom pictures/images for categories. Color Customization: Choose custom background (colorHex) and chart (chartColorHex) hex colors. 5. 📅 Configurable Financial Year Engine Flexible Cycles (FinancialYearConfig): Supports standard cycles (e.g., 1 Apr – 31 Mar, 1 Jan – 31 Dec) or custom start/end dates. Auto-Labeling & Filtering: Dynamically computes labels (e.g., "FY 2025-26") and aggregates budgets within the active cycle. 6. 📊 Analytics, Charts & Interactive Calendar Dashboard Summary (HomeScreen): Real-time metrics for Income, Expenses, Net Balance, and budget progress bars. Interactive Calendar (CalendarScreen): Monthly grid with daily status indicators; click any day to inspect transactions. Visual Analytics (ChartsScreen): Category pie/donut charts, breakdown percentages, and monthly trend comparisons. Filterable Reports (ReportsScreen): Filter history by date ranges, categories, transaction type, or member. 7. 📑 Report Export Engine (ExportHelper) Multi-Format Exports: Generate formatted CSV spreadsheets, PDF documents with header summaries, and Excel (.xlsx) files. Android Intent Sharing: Instantly share reports via FileProvider (Email, WhatsApp, Drive, or direct print). 8. ☁️ Real-Time Cloud Sync & Offline Architecture Firebase Integration: Uses Firestore and Realtime Database for instant multi-device sync and live edit updates. Offline-First Architecture: Queue transaction entries/deletions offline using PendingOfflineTransaction buffers (UPSERT/DELETE). Network Monitor & Sync Status: Tracks connectivity states (SYNCED, SYNCING, OFFLINE, ERROR), automatically flushes queues when online, and displays visual indicators (SyncStatusIcon). 9. 🔔 Notifications & System Alerts (AppNotificationHelper) Android Notification Channels: Dedicated channels for budget threshold warnings and family management updates. System Alerts: Triggers alerts when approaching budget limits or when family membership requests are submitted or approved.
